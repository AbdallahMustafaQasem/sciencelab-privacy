Science Lab - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Science Lab (package aq.store.a3d) is an educational app with interactive science simulations. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools. The app runs fully offline.

Data kept on your device

The app saves a few settings locally on your device only, such as your chosen language and your rating-prompt preferences. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app does not request any special Android permissions.

Rating and sharing

If you choose to rate the app, Google Play's in-app review is used and is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. If you choose to share the app or send feedback, your device's share sheet or email app opens and you control what is sent. The QR code in the app is generated on your device.

Children

The app contains no ads, no external links except those you tap, and collects no data, so it is suitable for students of all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
